## Future record format

For future additions, use a consistent structure: experiment title and number, aim, theory and equations, circuit or block diagram, apparatus and settings, procedure, raw observations, calculations, results, discussion and references.

Keep raw measurements distinct from calculated values. Include units and instrument ranges where relevant. Do not fabricate missing observations or results.

## Scope

This archive preserves the records and assets found in the source repositories. Some original repositories contain only a written procedure or README; no missing diagrams, raw observations, or experimental results have been invented. Inclusion does not imply independent verification or production-level validation.
