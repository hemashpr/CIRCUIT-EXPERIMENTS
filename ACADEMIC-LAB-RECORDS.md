## Record quality and conventions

For future additions, keep each experiment in a consistent format:

1. **Experiment title and number**
2. **Aim and objectives**
3. **Theory and governing equations**
4. **Circuit / block diagram and component or instrument details**
5. **Procedure**
6. **Observation table and raw data**
7. **Calculations and plots**
8. **Result and discussion**
9. **Sources / references**

Keep raw observations separate from calculated values. Include units, instrument ranges, calibration details where relevant, and enough information for another student to reproduce the work.

## Scope

This is an academic record and index of coursework. It is not a claim that every experiment has been independently validated or that every implementation is production-ready. Original repositories remain the source of their detailed files and commit history.
